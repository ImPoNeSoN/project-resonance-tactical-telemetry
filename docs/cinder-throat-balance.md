## Who is still on the edge

Korrith is the largest swing. The ten parties that include him average 13.0/24. The five that do not average 8.6/24. Zeph is next: 12.7/24 with him, 9.2/24 without him. Saeli and Kaelis sit slightly below the field once the tanked double-physical parties are no longer clearing every seed.

No hero is an automatic win or an automatic loss. The coldest four is Saeli, Kaelis, Aurel, and Seraphine at 6/24, on the 25% line, and it is the no-tank pair that used to clear 12/12. The hottest non-preset fours are Korrith/Saeli/Kaelis/Zeph and Korrith/Saeli/Zeph/Aurel at 17/24. Guardbreak is 18/24, on the 75% line, and remains 9/12 inside the preset floor band.
